This page summarizes the first full SQ2 flank-multiplicity screen across 136 squamate species. It asks how often SQ2 calls sit in nearly identical left-and-right genomic flank contexts, which can signal tandem arrays, assembly duplication, haplotigs, segmental duplication, or other copy-number inflation risks.

How To Read This Page

  • Normal singleton loci are SQ2 loci whose pair of flanks was unique under this threshold. These are the expected baseline for dispersed SINE insertions.
  • Abnormal clustered loci are SQ2 loci in components of two or more loci where both flanks are similar enough to connect the loci. These loci need inspection before using the raw SINEderella count as a clean independent-copy count.
  • The species summary table aggregates the three SQ2 rows per species. The species x SQ2 table keeps long, short, and unassigned SQ2 rows separate.
  • The downloadable TSV files include the full 408-row matrix and the 136-row species aggregate. This HTML page shows totals, definitions, method flags, and the highest-priority rows.

Category Definitions

CategoryRule used by the scriptHow to interpret it
Tandem / satellite candidateAll loci in the component are on one contig and the component span is <= 2 kb.This is the strongest signal for tandemly repeated SQ2-derived sequence or local repeat collapse. It is biologically interesting, but it can inflate naive copy counts.
Same-contig local clusterAll loci in the component are on one contig, but the span is > 2 kb and < 1 Mb.These loci have near-identical flanks but are not in the tight tandem/satellite window. Treat as local duplication, local assembly duplication, or nearby repeated sequence until inspected.
Same-contig segmental clusterAll loci in the component are on one contig and the span is >= 1 Mb.This is compatible with long-range segmental duplication or an assembly structure that placed duplicated sequence on one scaffold.
Dispersed cross-contig clusterThe component spans multiple contigs and does not match the sequential-contig heuristic.This is the largest broad category. It can represent duplicated haplotypes, segmental duplication, duplicated assembly contigs, or genuine dispersed genomic duplication of the flanking context.
Suspicious sequential-contig clusterThe component spans multiple contigs whose names share a prefix/suffix and differ by a small serial number.This is a heuristic flag for possible adjacent haplotigs, primary-contig segmentation, or assembly bookkeeping artifacts. It is not proof of error by itself.

Flag or limitationMeaning

Genome-boundary filteringLoci too close to contig ends to provide both 50 bp flanks are excluded from analyzed_loci and counted as incomplete_flanks.
Low-complexity filteringFlanks containing non-ACGT bases or dominated by one base above 70% are excluded, because they create weak evidence for locus identity.
